Privacy Statement (Student Work Collection)

We are collecting student work samples as part of our ongoing assessment and teaching programmes, in line with the New Zealand Curriculum (Te Mātaiaho). These samples may be used to support teaching decisions, moderation discussions, or professional development. All identifiable information will be kept private and secure. Student work will not be shared publicly without explicit permission from the student’s whānau.

This process aligns with the Privacy Act 2020 and Ministry of Education guidelines on student information.
